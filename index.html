IN ORDER TO BE THE HEROIC DEFENDER OF CLEAN WATER! YOUR MISSION IS SIMPLE... BUT NOT TOO SIMPLE.

BUT BE CAREFUL — ONLY CATCH THE HAPPY, CLEAN DROPS!
THE GRUMPY, POLLUTED DROPS? LET THEM FALL... OR IT'S GAME OVER!

AS TIME GOES ON, THE DROPS WILL FALL FASTER AND FASTER —
SO KEEP YOUR EYES SHARP AND YOUR BUCKET READY.

STAY FOCUSED, STAY HYDRATED, AND MAY THE CLEAN WATER BE WITH YOU!
